# Acknowledgements

People who shaped pitmaster, and what they did.

- **[cwlls](https://github.com/cwlls)** noticed that makeadr amended existing ADRs without saying it had chosen
  to, and suggested it say which ADR a change clarifies. makeadr now does, in one line, before it writes an
  amendment (0.4.30; ADR-005, amendment of 2026-10-29). They also asked whether a bbq run is meant to end in an
  ADR, which became ADR-036 (0.4.45), and they created the **napkin** skill, which starts a bbq from your rough
  design specs and asks only what they leave open (0.4.46; ADR-037).
- **[Humbedooh](https://github.com/Humbedooh)** suggested putting example prompts in the README, and wrote the
  first two. They became the examples in "Your first five minutes" (0.4.31).
